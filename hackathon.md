The Pitch: Redefining Retail
The Hook: The High-Stakes Guessing Game

Every single year, businesses pour lakhs of rupees into building and designing physical retail spaces. We design beautiful layouts on paper, and then we guess. We guess where the aisles should go, we guess where the promotional displays belong, and we guess how human beings will walk through the space.
But currently, the only way to truly test a layout hypothesis is to physically build it. And you know what? We often get it wrong.
When you get it wrong in the physical world, it is a high-stakes nightmare. Your profit margins are squeezed, immense customer disruption, heavy staff labor, and massive lost sales during rearrangement. If a layout fails, the cost of tearing it down and reverting it is astronomical.
Without behavioural testing, it is nearly impossible to predict dead zones, unexpected customer congestion, or checkout bottlenecks until real shoppers enter the space. Merchandisers have been stuck in a blind spot, relying purely on intuition to predict cross-sell and upsell micro-behaviours.
It’s slow. It’s expensive. And honestly? It’s completely unnecessary.
Today, we are going to change that. We are introducing a digital twin architecture that eliminates the guesswork entirely.
We call it RetailSim AI.

The Solution: From Blueprint to Business Intelligence

RetailSim AI is a behavioral digital twin platform that transforms static space planning into an interactive, data-driven feedback loop. It allows expert retail designers to stress-test layouts, validate product placements, and predict shopper behaviors before moving a single physical shelf.
We do this in four beautifully simple steps:
Spatial Ingestion: We map out the physical constraints the aisles, shelves, high-margin endcaps, and exits.
Digital Twin Generation: The system instantly synthesises a high-fidelity virtual replica of the environment.
Agent-Based Behavioral Simulation: We don't just simulate random dots on a screen. We use Generative AI to create hundreds of autonomous, highly complex shopper agents with realistic decision-making logic.
Predictive Analytics: We analyze the synthetic journeys to confidently lock in the highest-ROI layout before building.
These AI personas aren't just algorithms. They have identities. They have specific demographics, distinct financial details, strict shopping lists, and most importantly, secret impulse lists. They get frustrated by traffic bottlenecks and tempted by impulse items, behaving exactly like real human beings.

But we didn’t stop there. We wanted to solve two very specific, incredibly hard problems in retail: How do you accurately see where the friction is, and how do you guarantee a new product launch actually gets noticed?
To do this, we engineered two breakthrough software features.
Firstly, we created a feature that is going to change how merchandisers work forever. We call it the Retail Speed Bump.
Imagine you have a high-margin new product. Typically, you guess where to put it. With the Retail Speed Bump feature, you can drop a promotional zone or an aggressive end-cap display anywhere in this virtual layout.
You can see precisely how it alters their walking path, how it creates a "speed bump" that increases visibility, and whether it successfully triggers an impulse buy or just causes a frustrating bottleneck.
You can test 50 different locations for a new product launch or store layout in a single afternoon, finding the mathematical "sweet spot" for maximum conversion before moving a single physical shelf
Secondly, we built an advanced Heatmap Feature. As these hundreds of AI shoppers move through your store layout, the system aggregates their journeys in real-time. It paints a vivid visual map right over your layout. Instantly, you can spot the dead zones where no one goes, the high-traffic goldmines, and the exact spots where shoppers get bottlenecked or frustrated. It gives you X-ray vision into the customer experience.
Integrating both of these capabilities provides an exceptionally clear and accurate model of shopper behavior.

Customer Journey
Here everything begins with your business goal. You tell us what you want to improve whether it's increasing sales, optimizing shelf space, or testing a new store layout.
Next, you share your store information, and we create a digital version of your store.
We run simulations and analyze how shoppers interact with your current layout.
You then receive a clear report with insights and recommendations, and compare different layout options before making any changes.
If you'd like to explore more ideas, we test additional layouts and compare the results.
Finally, you choose the layout that delivers the best business outcome, and we provide the final recommendation along with the expected impact.
